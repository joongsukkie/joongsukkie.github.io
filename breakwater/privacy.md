# Privacy Policy for Breakwater

Last updated: 28 September 2026

## The short version

Breakwater does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Nothing you do in this app leaves your device.

## What the app stores, and where

Breakwater saves your settings, meaning your blocking mode, schedule, daily budget, the wording you chose for the shield screen, and your streak count, in a private App Group container on your device. This is shared only between Breakwater and its own extensions. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Your app selection

When you choose which apps and websites to block, you do so through Apple's own FamilyActivityPicker. Apple deliberately returns these selections as opaque tokens: Breakwater is given a handle it can pass back to the system, not the name or identity of anything you picked. This app cannot determine which apps you selected, and neither can its developer.

## Screen Time permission

Breakwater requests Screen Time, also called Family Controls, authorization so it can shield apps on your behalf. This permission is used only to apply the restrictions you configure. Breakwater does not read, aggregate, or report your usage statistics.

## The Safari content blocker

Breakwater includes a Safari content blocker. Content blockers in iOS work by handing Safari a static list of rules, which Safari applies itself. The extension is never told which pages you visit, and it has no ability to observe your browsing.

## Notifications

Breakwater does not send notifications and does not alter the notification settings of any other app.

## Third parties

Breakwater contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash-reporting service. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

## Children

Breakwater does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
