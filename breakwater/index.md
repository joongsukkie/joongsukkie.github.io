# Breakwater Support

Breakwater helps you limit your own use of distracting apps. It uses Apple's Screen Time controls to shield apps you choose, all day, on a schedule, or once a daily budget you set has been spent.

## Getting started

First, open Breakwater and approve the Screen Time permission when iOS asks. The app cannot block anything without it.

Next, tap Apps to block and pick the apps you want shielded.

Then turn on Block the apps I chose.

## Common questions

### Why can't it block only the Reels tab inside Instagram?

iOS does not let any app change or hide part of another app, and Apple grants no exceptions. Breakwater shields the whole app. On the web, Safari rules can be more precise, so the Messages only setting keeps instagram.com/direct working while the feed and Reels stay blocked.

### Will I still get message notifications?

Yes. Shielding an app does not silence it, and Breakwater never changes your notification settings. Opening a notification will land on the shield, so the app also offers a one-tap route to your messages in Safari.

### Why is the shortest break 15 minutes?

iOS will not schedule a monitoring window shorter than 15 minutes, so a shorter break could not be reliably ended. Breakwater does not promise one.

### How do I turn the Safari blocker on?

Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Extensions, and enable Breakwater.
